# Project title
Retail sales data agent

# Project objective
The objective of this is to help me as an analyst to ask business questions in plain language without requiring advanced SQL or technical expertise.

# Tools used
Databricks

# Dataset overview
The dataset that I’m working on is “retail sales” dataset. This dataset has 9 columns, namely, Transaction ID, Date, Customer ID, Gender, Age, Product Category, Quantity, Price per Unit, Total Amount. 

# Your agent instructions
You are a Retail Sales Analytics AI assistant designed to help business users analyze retail sales data accurately and efficiently.

Your Role
You help users:
Analyze retail sales performance
Identify trends and anomalies
Compare products, age and time periods
Generate business insights
Answer natural language questions using the retail dataset
Produce accurate SQL queries when required
Explain findings in simple business language

Constraints: 
Never fabricate data, metrics, or calculations.
Do not assume the existence of columns or tables.
Only use approved retail sales tables and views.

# Sample questions tested
1.	What were total sales in the last month?
2.  Which day had the highest revenue?
3.  Show weekly sales trends for the last 12 weeks.
4.  Compare sales last 2 months on a bar graph.
5.  What are the top-performing products and what are the least performing?
6.  Who are the top customers by revenue by age?
7.  Which product categories are growing fastest?
8.  Which moth has the highest sales and which month has the lowest sales?
9.  Which product category is the cheapest and which one is expensive?
10. What I want to understand is, since the age group 45-54 contribute the most, what they like to buy. Or is most bought product by the age group 45-54. 

# Key insights 
The Retail Sales Agent analyzed the retail_sales dataset and revealed several important business insights related to sales performance, customer behavior based on age, product trends, and profitability.

The retail sales agent was able to perform what was expected of it i.e to answer questions posed. What was challenging is that I had to specify the colours or different colours to be used in a graph. In the future, it should be able to forecast sales and include product demand predictions.
